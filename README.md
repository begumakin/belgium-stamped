# Belgium, Stamped

Stamp every Belgian municipality you've been to. All 565 municipalities as of 1 January 2025.

- Tap a municipality and mark it as **Stamped** (been there) or **Want to go**. Add a date or a note if you like.
- Use the filters to see only your stamps or only your want-to-go list.
- Your map is saved in your own browser. There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Share my map** makes a link that shows your map, read-only, to anyone who opens it.
- **Download backup** / **Restore** move your map between devices.
- On a phone, use *Add to Home Screen* to get it as an app.

There's also a printable A2 version (`belgie-gemeenten-A2.pdf`). Page 1 is a blank boundary map for colouring in, page 2 is a named index.

## Data

- Municipality boundaries: [mathiasleroy/belgium-geographic-data](https://github.com/mathiasleroy/belgium-geographic-data) (MIT). These are the 2019 boundaries, dissolved into the 2025 municipalities.
- Municipality names and the 2025 merger list: FPS BOSA BeST Address via [lekoala/belgian-geography](https://github.com/lekoala/belgian-geography), CC BY 4.0.

The boundaries are simplified. They're fine for a personal map but not survey-accurate.
